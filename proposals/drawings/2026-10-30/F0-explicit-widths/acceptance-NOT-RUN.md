## Owner-authorized changes from v3

Nineteen of the 24 old helper test bodies remain literal byte matches.
Four old helper bodies intentionally follow Cam's resolved explicit-W policy:
prepared default-width scalar requirements disappear, serialized DW750 becomes
absence of DW with unchanged explicit rows, and the old missing-GID0 refusal
becomes explicit-row success. The existing full-u16 case is strengthened:
parse all 65,536 rows, verify each CID exactly once and every finite literal
width (CID0=750, others277.83203125 in that synthetic component fixture), and
retain one-extra refusal. The component fixture's synthetic explicit GID0 is
not a claim that the real B1 producer emits it.

Three new helper declarations:
- `explicit_widths_cover_sparse_extreme_cids_without_a_notdef_record`: exact
  CID1/65535 rows, 131072 map bytes/endpoints, ToUnicode, original program bytes.
- `an_explicit_zero_cid_cannot_borrow_a_width_from_the_program_notdef`:
  distinct missing intrinsic-width and wrong CID0 mapping refusals survive.
- `actual_f0_producer_plan_emits_complete_explicit_width_resources`: real
  pinned paper_text -> Scene.add_fonts/prepare -> TextPlan -> opaque production
  font_objects for AV; absent B1 GID0 versus present program0 mapping, exact
  original-derived A/V widths, six refs, program bytes and ToUnicode. This is
  proposed source, not an executed producer or native result.

The five-case owned production-boundary fragment keeps its first four bodies
unchanged; only the prior unresolved-DW refusal is replaced by owner-authorized
successful resource emission. Thirteen retained B1 and four F0 wrapper cases,
plus all four negative obligations, are byte-identical to v3. The negative
snippets are intentional invalid boundary examples and must remain separate
from ordinary module code; their diagnostic classes are unobserved.
