# Proposed protected progressive work envelope — decision required before code

Recommendation: add a SEPARATE opt-in admitted protected-feedback policy that keeps the current ordinary-work cap B and admits a finite presentation allowance Q inside the SAME cumulative ledger. Explicitly publish and validate its aggregate ceiling B+Q. Do not silently enlarge the existing MAX_CPU_WORK, lower the control's cap, or alter frozen A2 IF1/V2. This proposal is not approval to implement, integrate or execute anything.

Engineer requested this design after the source-only A2 V2 review. A2's accepted shared-cap behavior remains unchanged: optional snapshots can exhaust later tracing/final work. V2 closes the unrelated missing full-pass tick at source only; all candidate tests and layouts remain uncompiled/unrun. No Python model, Rust, benchmark, Cargo, formatter, application or numerical experiment was executed for this proposal. Reasoning below is algebra and direct source inspection.

## Actual source and the unavoidable tradeoff

Current WorkBudget in raytrace/mod.rs:66-92 has limit/used; ordinary tick delegates to charge, charge checks the fixed total and cancellation, and remaining=limit-used. The producer constructor selects its current CpuBudget max_work_units. Compact=1000000000, Studio=1000000000000, Workstation=16000000000000. The original MAX_CPU_WORK is16000000000000. CPU admission separately limits dimensions, camera samples, memory and selected profile; it does not reserve final WORK. Existing final leases reserve MEMORY.

A2 V2 begin:1604-1623 accepts if remaining>=W, then charges W=12M+65538 before allocation. It does not know the work needed by untraced rays or protect final resolution/encoding. Ordinary final still owes pass transition, linear resolve, encoder setup/rows/finish and PNG handoff scan. Host sidecar/hash are part of W, not another debit. Actual scene transport cost depends on traversal, shadow rays, lights, material selection, environment sampling and bounce/null paths. Progress fraction, sample count and elapsed time are not a remaining-work certificate.

Let an unchanged control need T ordinary units and let accepted snapshots cost V>0. If T=B, unchanged total ceiling B cannot also admit T+V. Separate counters do not solve that inequality. Splitting B into ordinary(B-Q) and presentation(Q) reduces the previous control's cap; that must never be described as preserving it. A guarantee of constant250ms updates for arbitrarily long work is likewise incompatible with a finite snapshot quota of positive W. Requests may be throttled at250ms; publication frequency is a different claim.

## Alternatives and decision

| Policy | Ordinary cap and aggregate | Guarantee and cost |
|---|---|---|
| Frozen A2 V2 | Ordinary/presentation share B | Existing increment; can fail solely because of accepted presentation. Not sufficient for protected long-final acceptance. |
| Fixed split of existing B | Ordinary B-Q, presentation Q, total B | Implementable and finite, but does not preserve the previous control cap. Reject as an equivalent fix. |
| Certified slack plus finite quota | Ordinary cap B, total B; require proven future ordinary bound R before spending | Preserves original limits and control completion when the proof covers the route. Unknown proof must decline feedback honestly. Full admitted opaque/HDR/reflection/shadow bound is not delivered here; may provide few/no previews for expensive scenes. |
| Explicit protected envelope, RECOMMENDED | Ordinary B unchanged; presentation Q; total G=B+Q under a new explicit finite aggregate policy | Preserves ordinary control allowance and prevents budget starvation by feedback, at the cost of explicitly allowing more total work. Requires Root approval, real admission, new accounting/metadata and actual acceptance. |

The last alternative follows Engineer's explicit design steering: a published new aggregate policy is allowed as an option to approve, not a silent change to current caps. It is the smallest general way to provide both a protected old ordinary allowance and useful finite feedback without an unproved scene estimator. This is not yet permission to raise any cap in code.

## Concrete candidate quota, not a claimed optimum

For a FIRST REVIEWED protected policy, propose at most K=64 accepted snapshots per job, including all in-flight attempts whose W debit succeeded. K is an explicit policy candidate requiring approval, not a measured optimal value or a request-rate promise. For a fixed admitted preview plan, Q=checked(K*W), W=checked(12M+65538). Each accepted attempt costs exactly the same W as A2. No refund for cancellation/encoding/allocation failure after debit. No unused quota transfer, retry reset, second pool or background job.

For the unchanged max-edge1024 plan, M<=1048576, W<=12648450, Q<=809500800. The NEW aggregate upper bound would be explicitly `MAX_CPU_WORK + 809500800 = 16000809500800`, while MAX_CPU_WORK keeps its old meaning as the ordinary cap. Each request's actual aggregate is B(profile)+Q(plan), not that maximum for every request. All arithmetic/conversions must be checked before task allocation. New constants require documentation and approval; WorkBudget::new currently rejects that new maximum, so a distinct validated constructor/envelope is necessary. Do not weaken that legacy constructor to accept arbitrary bigger numbers.

Worst-case logical presentation allowance is about81% of Compact's ordinary cap at a1024-square preview. This is substantial and must not be hidden. It is less relative to larger ordinary profiles, but logical-unit ratios are not elapsed-time ratios. K64 may fail usability/performance acceptance even though it protects completion; measurement may require a smaller K or preview plan in a newly reviewed policy. A proposal must not be called optimal merely because it is finite.

These are algebraic logical-work bounds, not benchmark results. A lower preview resolution reduces presentation cost while retaining source dimensions, final target samples and final quality. It would require a NEW agreed plan/version across admission, producer, host and receiver; changing IF1's fixed fit behind existing callers is prohibited. Keep the current plan for the minimum protected-envelope implementation unless Engineer explicitly chooses that larger interface change.

K must be finite independently of how often UI calls request. Admit no more successful debits once K is consumed. To avoid spending all K early, use deterministic source-progress eligibility, not render wall clock: an early first safe observation, then finite admitted milestones distributed over remaining camera commits. A concrete bounded candidate is K_eff=min(64,P*S-1), with first milestone1 and, if K_eff>1, milestone_j=1+floor(j*(P*S-2)/(K_eff-1)), j0..K_eff-1. For P*S<=1, K_eff=0. Every multiplication and sentinel conversion is checked. Only safe changed represented frontiers below target may satisfy a milestone; missed milestones coalesce into ONE observation and are never replayed as a burst. Exact milestone/cadence policy needs owner approval; it is separate from the ledger proof.

Progress milestones avoid prematurely exhausting the quota but may leave a long expensive pass with sparse feedback. They do not guarantee a screenshot every250ms. A shorter interval would not create additional quota or CPU throughput. Request cadence remains the existing monotonic UI minimum250ms, with false attempts throttled; host/producer still do not use clocks or RNG keys based on time. Final-target progress has priority and does not wait for an observer or for unused quota to be spent.

## One ledger: required invariants and proof

One owned WorkBudget/envelope carries O ordinary-used, V presentation-used, B ordinary-limit, Q presentation-limit, G aggregate-limit. Invariants are checked before mutation:

`used = O+V; O<=B; V<=Q; G=B+Q; used<=G`.

All counters are monotonic. Existing ordinary preparation, BVH, trace, pass-transition, film, final resolve/encoder/hash-handoff charges go to O with their EXACT unchanged amounts. Existing builder internal counters remain internal bounds; image mirrors its actual build delta once into the same O, as now. A new explicit presentation charge(W) goes to V ONCE at snapshot BEGIN before allocation, under the already acquired host credit and real cancellation check. Existing prepaid continuations/host verification/hash do not debit again. Do not relabel normal transition/final work as presentation or subtract any overhead from used.

An ordinary charge d is admitted only if O+d<=B and O+V+d<=G; a presentation charge W only if V+W<=Q, successful-attempt count<K and O+V+W<=G. Validate ALL sums and cancellation first, then update the same object atomically at the source level. No independently spendable WorkBudget, global pool, credit refund, periodic reset, negative accounting, work forgone assertion or separate unbounded codec allowance is introduced.

Expose explicit ordinary_remaining, presentation_remaining and aggregate_remaining to prevent confusing the new policy with A2's single remaining value. Keep legacy new(B), used(), remaining() and charge/tick semantics unchanged for legacy None; if used() is reported on a protected job it must mean total O+V, with separate ordinary/presentation fields available. Do not let a generic aggregate remaining check replace the presentation quota or the ordinary cap.

Proof: compare identical requests using the NEW protected policy, one requesting snapshots and the other not. Assume identical source, plan, seed, absolute camera ordinals, normal charges and all other resources/cancellation outcomes; A2's corrected freeze/resume is retained. If the no-feedback control completes with T<=B, the feedback run owes those same T ordinary units. Since accepted V<=Q, at every ordinary prefix O+d<=T<=B and O+d+V<=B+Q=G. Thus presentation cannot cause an ordinary WorkLimit or steal final-work headroom. Its own quota exhaustion suppresses feedback and tracing/final continue. A control that itself exceeds B still fails honestly. This is a budget proof, not a guarantee against allocation failure, invalid input, cancellation, thread starvation or arbitrary OS/GPU failures.

The guarantee MUST also be compared against the old partial-enabled no-snapshot control at B: old and new protected ordinary sequences/caps must match, including final. A legacy power-of-two-preview control is a different workload, so its checkpoint charges cannot silently enter that equivalence oracle. New aggregate construction may need larger struct fields; keep and re-evaluate all ImageTask/FRAME/coordinator capacity guards on actual target builds. Counting metadata never establishes memory admission by itself.

## Admission, exhaustion and scope contract

New mode must be chosen explicitly through an agreed additive route/envelope; existing A2 IF1 and V2, legacy None/default submission, saved format and current profile meaning remain unchanged. A protected mode cannot silently claim to be the old shared-cap policy. Producer/host admission must receive the identical checked B,Q,G,plan,K before constructing the task. Existing CPU memory pool, two preview slots, single encoder, exact capacities, final separate lease, demand coalescing and destruction-before-credit rules remain required.

Provide a dedicated additive read-only admitted-work report (names to agree with owners) with policy/version, B,Q,G,O,V and quota exhaustion. Legacy/default output must remain compatible. Make aggregate cost visible in documentation and diagnostics; user-facing controls should describe the meaningful tradeoff of bounded feedback versus final-only, without exposing internal symbols as normal workflow steps. Any persisted preference/command change follows its normal project format/schema/default rules; this proposal does not assign those files.

Distinguish: waiting for safe frontier/milestone/credit, quota exhausted, and ordinary-work failure. Waiting is temporary; it must NOT permanently latch exhaustion merely because a conservative bound or progress milestone might become eligible later. Quota exhaustion is final for presentation in this job and does not set job Failed or prevent final publication. Cancellation remains terminal; failures after a successful W debit remain charged, and memory ownership follows the existing slot rules. Final cannot be blocked by two external preview holders, remaining demand, a milestone or unspent Q.

## Certified-slack alternative: where it is and is not proved

Under unchanged total B, keep the same ordinary/presentation accounting but accept W only if `O+V+W+R(state)<=B`, V+W<=Q and count<K. R must upper-bound ALL future ordinary work of the same no-feedback route from the captured safe state through successful final, not just tracing or camera commits. Arithmetic overflow/unsupported proof means temporarily ineligible feedback, not an invented R. Reserving B-O for R gives zero slack and no useful feedback; that safe fallback alone does not meet the user's progress objective.

A source-derived restricted example exists for a fully prepared EMPTY BVH, ready film, no pending ray/encoder, no optical/material/environment transport and primary_samples1. Every remaining primary miss costs exactly three ordinary ticks: ray setup, empty TraceCursor.step and film.add. At safe frontier(s,k), let N=(S-s)*P-k remaining camera commits and J=(S-s)+I(k0 and cursorP) future pass-transition ticks. Final remaining resolve/setup/row/finish costs2P+2 and actual PNG handoff length is bounded by the existing source encoded_limit Csrc. Therefore R_empty=3N+J+2P+2+Csrc is a conservative residual bound. It includes the nonfinal sentinel tick fixed in V2 and final's transition in J. It is not a bound for a nonempty scene; primary misses there can still visit arbitrarily many admitted nodes/triangles.

For general admitted geometry, a proof needs actual traversal node/primitive bounds, every light/shadow/effect/emission/environment stage, reflection/null/bounce multiplicity, remaining camera samples and final codec-length bound. BVH MAX_DEPTH alone does not bound work: a capped-depth leaf can contain many primitives. Neither observed average work per ray, elapsed time nor a few test results proves an upper bound. No complete route proof is supplied here. A full certified estimator would be more invasive than the explicit admitted-quota ledger and can be too pessimistic to deliver useful previews. Treat it as a later optimization/unchanged-cap option, not the recommended general fix.

## Original counterexample specification — NOT RUN

Use an original empty-scene procedural32x32 CPU progressive request, S64, no lights/HDR/reflections/resources, fixed seed and background[10,20,30]. P=M1024, total real camera samples65536, W77826. No third-party asset, fake work loop, synthetic film mutation, shader mock or skipped tracer is needed.

Construct a no-feedback control in the current partial policy, run its real ordinary path to completion under the existing profile, and record T=actual control work. This is a PROPOSED later Rust regression, not a result from this analysis. Reconstruct TWO fresh identical tasks with limit T BEFORE starting them: one no-feedback control, one taking exactly one snapshot at the actual first committed pixel(s0,k1). Do not reset used or change limit mid-render. At that frontier, future ordinary camera work alone is at least3*(65536-1)=196605>W, so the snapshot preflight can succeed under T. The no-feedback task still needs exactly T and completes; the snapshot task owes T+W and must return WorkLimit before publishing a final. Its normal final requirement did not disappear when W was charged. Verify analytic all-miss final RGBA and film/coverage/counts on the control; never change expectations to match a candidate.

This proves the shared-cap counterexample for a finite actual task and a test-boundary limit, without needing a million-pixel image or executing anything now. It is not a claim of a failure observed under the full production Compact cap or an actual250ms UI schedule. The host API also permits many coalesced requests over changed safe frontiers, but a real production cadence/failure claim would need the actual UI/host run. Do not infer it from this source fixture.

For protected mode, reconstruct the same fixture with ordinary B=T and admitted Q=64W, while requesting exactly ONE snapshot. The smaller ordinary limit is a test-boundary constructor, not an arbitrary public production envelope; real production B remains the selected profile's original B. Both complete with identical final pixels, linear bits, sample counts and primary-ray log; ordinary_used must match T and total_used must equal T+W for that one accepted snapshot. This directly tests preservation of final work and rejects an implementation that silently lowers ordinary cap to T-W, stops charging presentation, refunds W or repeats a sample. A separate policy test covers K64/profile aggregate admission and exhausting all64 attempts; do not make this boundary fixture imply production performance.
